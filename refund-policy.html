Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Applies to jyogi.in

This policy covers paid consultations and digital reports booked through jyogi.in and paid via Razorpay. Payments are verified manually before a booking is confirmed.

1. Cancellation by you

You may cancel a consultation up to 24 hours before the scheduled time for a full refund. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled time are not eligible for a refund, as the slot has been reserved for you.

2. Rescheduling

You may reschedule a booked consultation once, free of charge, if you request it at least 12 hours before the scheduled time. Later reschedule requests are accommodated where possible but are not guaranteed.

3. No-show

If you miss a confirmed consultation without notice, the session is treated as delivered and is not refundable. If Jyogi is unable to attend a confirmed session, you will be offered a reschedule or a full refund.

4. Digital reports

Digital reports (such as the PDF included with a Full Vedic Reading) are prepared individually for you. Once a report has been prepared and delivered, it is non-refundable. If you cancel before the report has been prepared, you are eligible for a full refund.

5. Duplicate payments

If you are charged more than once for the same booking, the duplicate amount is refunded in full. Please contact us with both payment references.

6. Failed or pending payments

If a payment fails, no booking is created. If your bank, card or UPI provider debits an amount for a failed or pending transaction, the return of that amount is handled by your provider and Razorpay under their own processes and timelines. Jyogi does not control and cannot guarantee whether or when such an amount is reversed. If you believe you were charged for a failed payment, contact us with your payment reference and we will help you follow it up with Razorpay.

7. How refunds are processed

Where a refund is approved, Jyogi initiates it to your original payment method through Razorpay. The time it takes to reach you is determined by Razorpay and by your bank, card or UPI provider, and is outside Jyogi's control — Jyogi cannot guarantee a fixed completion date. We will share the Razorpay refund reference so you can track it with your provider.

8. How to request

To cancel, reschedule or request a refund, contact Jyogi on WhatsApp +91 94377 94561 with your name, selected service and Razorpay payment reference. We aim to respond within 24 hours.

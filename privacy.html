3. Information we do not collect directly

Jyogi does not request Aadhaar numbers or other government identification for ordinary consultations or reports. Jyogi does not receive or store your full card number, bank-login credentials, UPI PIN or UPI password. Payment credentials are handled within Razorpay's systems.

Jyogi does not create customer accounts and does not use website information for targeted advertising.

4. Free website reports and consultation reports

Free website reports are generated through an automated software process. When you request such a report, the website may transmit only the calculated astrological factors needed to prepare the output to a contracted report-processing service. Your name, email address, phone number and raw birth details are not intentionally included in that automated request.

Calculated astrological factors may still relate to you and are therefore handled as personal information where applicable. Do not enter confidential, financial, medical or government-identification information into a free-report text field.

Reports supplied with a paid consultation are different. They are prepared after Jyotirmoy Giri personally analyses and reviews the horoscope. They are not represented as an instant website-generated report.

5. Website infrastructure, security and analytics

Jyogi uses Cloudflare for website delivery and security and Render for server-side calculations and application hosting. These providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request metadata and server logs as needed to provide and secure their services.

The website loads Google Analytics in a restricted configuration for limited usage measurement. The current configuration requests IP anonymisation and disables client-side analytics storage. Google may nevertheless receive technical request information when the analytics script loads.

Processing by service providers is governed by their applicable contractual and privacy terms. Use of these providers does not by itself guarantee that every configuration or business process is compliant with every foreign privacy law.

6. Payments through Razorpay

Paid services on this site use Razorpay-hosted Payment Links. When you choose to pay, you leave jyogi.in and complete the payment on Razorpay's page. Razorpay processes the customer details entered on its page, together with the payment method, amount, payment status and transaction reference.

Jyogi may access limited booking and transaction information through the Razorpay Dashboard so that a payment can be matched to a booking, a receipt can be issued and a refund or support request can be handled.

Opening or reaching a payment-success page does not itself prove that payment was completed. During the current payment phase, Jyogi manually verifies payment in the Razorpay Dashboard and then confirms the booking directly. Payment does not automatically create a consultation booking or unlock a paid consultation report.

Razorpay's privacy policy: razorpay.com/privacy. Refund and cancellation terms: Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. WhatsApp and social-media communications

When you contact Jyogi through WhatsApp or Instagram, the information in that conversation is processed through the relevant platform and is subject to that platform's privacy terms. Jyogi does not copy every conversation into a separate customer database, but the message history may remain available in the relevant account until it is deleted or no longer reasonably required.

8. Retention and deletion

Website data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose, security, support, dispute handling or compliance with applicable law. Rate-limiting identifiers, where enabled, are retained for up to 90 days. Payment, invoice, refund and accounting records may be retained for the period required under applicable tax, accounting and legal obligations.

Information stored only in your browser can generally be removed by clearing the relevant browser storage. Deleting information held by a third-party provider may require a request to that provider or may be subject to legal retention obligations.
